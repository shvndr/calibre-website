The Beginning

Why We
Started Calibre

The Indian market for fine watches was either overpriced authorised retail or the anxious gamble of grey imports with no service safety net. There was no middle ground — no place where you could buy with confidence, read genuine editorial guidance, and know the watch was real.

We built Calibre to fill that gap. Our collection is deliberately small. Every watch on these pages has been personally handled, researched, and selected on the basis of mechanical merit, long-term value, and whether we'd actually wear it ourselves.

We are authorised partners for several brands and work with verified global distributors for the rest. Every piece ships with documentation, comes with our own 2-year service cover, and is ours to stand behind.

How We Work

Calibre — A Brief History
January 2024
Calibre is founded in Mumbai by two mechanical watch collectors frustrated with the existing market landscape.
March 2024
First collection launched — 12 watches, 8 brands. All sold out within 6 weeks. The waitlist begins.
July 2024
Formal authorised dealer status awarded for Hamilton, Longines, and Tissot. Service infrastructure established.
December 2024
Collection expands to 32 pieces across 20 brands. The strap programme launches alongside dedicated watch care guides.
2025 →
