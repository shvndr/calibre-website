# ⌚ Calibre - Watch Showcase Website

Welcome to **Calibre**, your one-stop destination for showcasing stunning watches! This project is designed with a **dark navy blue** and **golden theme**, perfect for our Diploma WPD subject project.

## 🌟 Product Gallery
Discover an exquisite collection of watches displayed in our beautiful product gallery.

![Product Gallery](link_to_image)

## 🔍 Filters
Easily navigate through our collection with the help of filters:
- **Brand**
- **Type**
- **Price Range**

## 🛒 Shopping Cart
Keep track of your selected watches in the shopping cart, where you can:
- Modify quantities
- Remove items
- Proceed to checkout

## 📱 Responsive Design
Our website is responsive, providing a seamless experience on both desktop and mobile devices:
- Full functionality across all devices
- User-friendly interface
